Current price of oil as of May 11, 2026

Staff May 12, 2026 5 min read

Crude oil markets continue to respond to a mix of supply-side pressures and demand signals as we enter the second week of May 2026. Both WTI and Brent benchmarks are trading within their recent ranges, reflecting ongoing uncertainty about OPEC+ production decisions and global economic growth. Traders remain focused on inventory data and geopolitical developments that could shift sentiment in either direction.

Refinery throughput in the United States and Europe has remained relatively stable, supporting crude demand at current levels. The summer driving season approach is beginning to influence market sentiment, though seasonal strength has been modest compared to historical patterns. Any significant shift in regional fuel demand could provide price direction in the coming weeks.

OPEC+ compliance with existing production agreements continues to be a key factor for price stability. Recent signals from the cartel suggest member states are managing output carefully, though compliance levels vary across the group. Any announcements regarding future production policy could trigger renewed volatility.

Downstream markets and shipping through critical chokepoints like the Strait of Hormuz remain normal, with no material supply disruptions reported. The contango structure in forward curves suggests traders expect gradual supply adequacy through the summer period. Market participants will be watching weekly crude inventory reports and any updates from major producing nations for directional cues.
