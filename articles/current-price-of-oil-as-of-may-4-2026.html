Current price of oil as of May 4, 2026

Staff May 5, 2026 5 min read

Crude oil markets are trading within familiar ranges as of early May 2026, with both WTI and Brent benchmarks reflecting ongoing supply-demand dynamics in a globally interconnected energy landscape. Traders continue to monitor geopolitical developments, OPEC+ production decisions, and macroeconomic indicators that shape price direction across sessions.

Any significant shifts in crude differentials or regional supply constraints typically filter through to retail energy prices within weeks.

Market participants are advised to monitor official EIA and IEA inventory reports, OPEC+ meeting outcomes, and shipping updates through the Strait of Hormuz for signals on near-term price direction.
