Oil price predicted to remain above $100 for rest of year

Staff May 11, 2026 5 min read

Market analysts point to persistent supply constraints and geopolitical tensions as anchors keeping crude prices elevated through the final months of 2024. The combination of OPEC+ production management and ongoing Middle East concerns has tightened global inventories, limiting downside risk for both WTI and Brent benchmarks. These structural factors are expected to maintain a floor beneath prices that keeps them from slipping below the three-digit threshold.

OPEC+ production decisions remain central to the price outlook. The cartel’s ability to maintain discipline on output cuts, coupled with voluntary reductions by key members, continues to support crude valuations. Any unexpected disruptions to supplies from major producers would likely reinforce the upside bias already embedded in current market pricing.

Seasonal refining demand typically strengthens as the year progresses, adding another layer of support to crude consumption forecasts. Winter heating requirements in the Northern Hemisphere and year-end operational needs across refineries globally are factoring into analyst expectations for sustained crude demand. This seasonal tailwind, combined with supply tightness, leaves little room for the market to soften materially.

The risk of price volatility remains elevated, particularly around geopolitical flashpoints and economic data releases. While the three-digit floor appears durable based on fundamentals, traders should monitor any shifts in OPEC+ policy, financial positioning by hedge funds, or unexpected changes in global growth expectations. Near-term swings are likely, but the structural support for prices above $100 appears entrenched for the remainder of the year.
