Oil price predicted to remain above $100 for rest of year

Staff May 14, 2026 5 min read

Several factors are currently supporting crude prices at elevated levels heading into the final months of 2024. Ongoing supply constraints from major producers, combined with persistent geopolitical tensions in key export regions, continue to underpin market sentiment. Analysts point to tighter global inventory levels and strong demand from Asian refineries as additional supports.

OPEC+ production decisions remain a central driver of price direction. The cartel’s ongoing output management strategy, combined with voluntary cuts from member states, has helped maintain supply discipline. Any deviation from announced production schedules could shift market expectations, though current guidance suggests continued restraint.

Demand-side dynamics remain mixed across major consuming regions. While economic growth concerns persist in some developed markets, energy demand from emerging economies continues to support consumption. A sustained global recession would pose downside risk to the price floor, though leading indicators have not signaled such a scenario at present.

Geopolitical risks in the Middle East and broader supply chain disruptions continue to warrant a premium in crude valuations. Market participants remain cautious about potential escalations that could affect transit through critical chokepoints. This risk premium, combined with the supply-demand balance outlined above, provides structural support for sustained higher price levels through year’s end.
