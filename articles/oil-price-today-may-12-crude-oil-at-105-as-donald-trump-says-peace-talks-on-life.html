Staff May 12, 2026 5 min read

West Texas Intermediate crude settled near $105 per barrel on Monday as geopolitical tensions weighed on market sentiment. Comments from former President Donald Trump regarding ongoing peace negotiations added uncertainty to an already volatile trading environment. Energy traders are closely monitoring developments in diplomatic efforts, which could influence supply chain stability and investor confidence in the months ahead.

Trump’s characterization of peace talks as being on “life support” has heightened concerns about prolonged geopolitical friction. Markets tend to price in risk premiums when diplomatic pathways narrow, particularly in regions critical to global oil supply. The Strait of Hormuz remains a focal point for traders assessing potential supply disruptions.

Crude prices have shown resilience near the $105 level despite mixed signals from demand data and OPEC+ production policies. The energy market continues to balance concerns about economic slowdown against supply constraints and geopolitical risks. Investors are watching for any changes in crude inventories and refinery utilization rates that could shift price direction.

Oil traders are positioning for continued volatility as political developments unfold. Short-term price action will likely depend on whether diplomatic tensions escalate further or show signs of de-escalation. Market participants are advised to monitor official statements and supply data releases closely for clues about near-term direction.
