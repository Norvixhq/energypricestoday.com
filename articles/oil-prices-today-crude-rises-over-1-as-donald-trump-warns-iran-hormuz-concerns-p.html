Oil prices today: Crude rises over 1% as Donald Trump warns Iran, Hormuz concerns persist

Staff May 15, 2026 5 min read

Crude oil futures advanced more than 1% in early trading, supported by escalating geopolitical tensions in the Middle East. The gains reflect ongoing market sensitivity to supply risks, particularly around one of the world’s most critical chokepoints for petroleum flows. Investors are monitoring rhetoric from U.S. leadership alongside developments that could affect regional stability.

The Strait of Hormuz remains a focal point for energy traders, with roughly one-fifth of global seaborne oil transiting through the narrow waterway daily. Any disruption to shipping lanes or production facilities in the Persian Gulf carries outsized weight in crude pricing. Market participants are assessing whether current tensions will translate into actual supply constraints or remain primarily a risk-premium dynamic.

WTI and Brent benchmarks have shown volatility in recent sessions as traders balance demand concerns against supply-side uncertainty. The oil market continues to digest mixed signals on global economic growth while remaining attuned to OPEC+ production decisions and compliance. Geopolitical developments, particularly those affecting major producers or transit routes, tend to command premium valuations during periods of uncertainty.

Energy analysts are watching for additional policy statements and military posturing that could either de-escalate or intensify near-term price pressure. The direction of crude will likely depend on whether these warnings materialize into concrete actions affecting crude flows. Until clearer resolution emerges, crude markets are expected to remain reactive to headline developments from the region.
