Current price of oil as of May 14, 2026

Staff May 14, 2026 5 min read

Crude oil benchmarks closed the trading session on May 14, 2026, reflecting ongoing dynamics in global supply and demand. Market participants continued to monitor geopolitical developments, inventory levels, and macroeconomic signals that drive price discovery in both WTI and Brent futures.

The energy market landscape remains shaped by OPEC+ production decisions and their impact on available barrels in the global market. Refinery utilization rates and seasonal demand patterns in key consuming regions also influence daily price movements, with traders adjusting positions based on near-term supply assessments.

Logistics considerations, including throughput at critical chokepoints and regional crude differentials, continue to be priced into spot and forward contracts. Weather patterns, maintenance schedules at major production facilities, and financial flows all contribute to the complexity of current price formation.

For market participants and energy consumers tracking exposure to crude oil, spot prices represent just one component of the broader investment and procurement landscape. Forward contracts, hedging strategies, and regional pricing spreads often matter more than single-day settlement levels when making operational or financial decisions.
